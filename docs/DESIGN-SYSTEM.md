# Design System

## Direction

The personal site combines restrained editorial typography, large photography, cinematic motion, and dark neutral surfaces. The interface should support the content rather than compete with it.

## Principles

- Photography is the primary visual asset.
- Text should remain readable over motion and backgrounds.
- Motion should feel patient and deliberate.
- Navigation must stay quiet and predictable.
- Personal content must never be obscured by effects.

## Layout

- Wide desktop canvas with generous whitespace
- Strong editorial headings
- Full-width media bands
- Compact mobile navigation
- Article reader with focused text measure

## Typography

- Display: large, high-contrast titles
- Body: 16-19px with comfortable line height
- Metadata: small uppercase or tabular numerals
- Chinese and Latin text must remain balanced

## Motion

- Slow image reveals
- Subtle depth movement
- Text highlighting tied to scroll
- Carousel transitions with reduced-motion fallback
- Music visualizer secondary to content

## Accessibility

- Keyboard navigation for articles and photos
- Focus-visible controls
- Alternate text for meaningful images
- Reduced-motion mode
- Mobile-safe touch targets
